Privacy Policy

Effective 29 September 2026

The short version: your photos never leave your iPhone unless you share them, and we don't track you.

What stays on your device

  • All photo processing is on-device. The camera looks, flash, grain, date stamps, photo booth strips and imports are computed on your iPhone with Apple's frameworks. No photo is sent to us or to any third-party service.
  • Your library (developed photos, the originals kept for re-developing, and booth strips) is stored in the app's private storage, protected by iOS Data Protection.
  • The home-screen widget shows small thumbnails of your latest photos, stored in a container shared only between RetroCam and its widget on your device.
  • Photos taken from the Lock Screen stay in a private folder on your device until RetroCam next opens and moves them into your library.

Permissions

  • Camera — to take photos. Used only while the camera is on screen.
  • Photos (add only) — to save your developed photos and strips to your library. RetroCam cannot read your library.
  • Importing uses Apple's photo picker, so RetroCam only receives the photos you choose.
  • Notifications (optional) — only if you turn on Develop Mode, to tell you when your photos are ready. They are scheduled on your device.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is processed by Apple. To validate it and restore it on your other devices, we use RevenueCat, which receives a random anonymous identifier, the purchase information Apple provides, and basic technical details (app version, OS version, device model, country or storefront). It never receives your photos, name or email from us.

Sharing

When you share or export a photo, it goes to the app or service you choose, and that destination's own policies apply.

Retention and deletion

Your photos stay until you delete them. Deleting a photo in RetroCam removes it from the app; copies already saved to Photos stay there. Deleting the app deletes its library.

Children

RetroCam is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
